The Story

Where two worlds flood together.

Sovereign Tide begins at the edge of a storm. The air is mineral and saline, alive with algae and the dark green scent of water moving over stone.

The tide reaches inland. Wet earth and mushroom surface beneath it, cool and organic, while driftwood gives the composition a weathered, silvered grain. Nothing is merely aquatic; the sea is made tactile by soil, roots, and decay becoming new life.

Sandalwood and resin bring composure to the final trail. The strange landscape becomes smooth, meditative, and unexpectedly luxurious.

Olfactory Journey

Storm, soil, stillness.

The fragrance moves from dark water to living earth, then resolves into polished woods and resin.
