The Story

A hard green light wrapped in velvet.

Velvet Jade begins cold, green, and composed. Bergamot, violet leaf, black tea, elemi, and pepper create the impression of polished clarity rather than conventional freshness.

The surface softens without surrendering its edge. Jasmine Auriculatum, Sambac, orris, mimosa, and ionones form a tactile floral center—less bouquet than velvet laid over cool stone.

Mysore sandalwood and olibanum deepen the structure while ambrette and refined musks move the fragrance toward skin. At distance it remains controlled; close up, the warmth becomes harder to ignore.

Olfactory Journey

Cold, tactile, intimate.

The architecture moves from green clarity through a soft floral texture into sandalwood, incense, and a persistent skin halo.

The scent in balance.

Velvet Jade is designed around contrast: crisp green radiance against tactile floral softness, dry sacred woods against a warmer skin-musk aura.

Emotional Signature
Silk skin. Cold heart.

Velvet Jade is designed to feel crisp, composed, and expensive at distance, then warmer, softer, and more sensual at close range.

Social Map

How distance becomes closeness.

The development hypothesis for Velvet Jade is fascination first: an unusual green tea–orris–jasmine construction that reads polished and expensive before sandalwood, ambrette, incense, and musk bring the wearer closer.

A creative development direction, not a guaranteed behavioral claim. Skin, dose, climate, setting, memory, and individual taste shape the final experience.
