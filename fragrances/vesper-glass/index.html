The Story

Metal becomes human.

At first, Vesper Glass does not feel alive. It arrives as something silver, smooth, and cold enough to hold the light.

The metallic edge slowly gives way to iris root, narcissus, black tea, and pale suede. Nothing changes abruptly. What seemed inorganic simply begins revealing something floral inside it.

Then the fragrance warms. Incense rises through sandalwood, ambergris, transparent woods, and musk until the polished object feels inhabited. What began as glass seems to have a pulse.

Olfactory Journey

Metal → flower → skin.

Vesper Glass is designed as one continuous transformation: surface becomes flower, flower becomes touch, and touch becomes warmth.

Nose Map

Cold precision, intimate finish.

The intended movement is from cool, smooth, and architectural toward tactile, warm, and human—without relying on sweetness to create sensuality.

Signature Materials

The visible architecture.

Iris Root

Cool, dry, root-like texture that allows the floral heart to feel architectural rather than decorative.

Black Tea

A tannic shadow that keeps the floral center dry, quiet, and disciplined.

Narcissus

A strange floral edge that helps the cold surface feel increasingly alive.

Pale Suede

Tactile softness between iris and skin—more fabric than heavy leather.

Incense

A fine smoky thread running through the transition into the warmer drydown.

Sandalwood & Ambergris

Soft mineral warmth that lets the final structure settle naturally against skin.

Emotional Signature
Precise. Enigmatic. Intimate.

Vesper Glass is intended to reward attention rather than demand it—the closer it moves to skin, the less certain the original coldness feels.

Social Map

How it changes at proximity.

The intended first impression is cool, immaculate, and difficult to categorize. At conversational distance the iris, tea, and suede become more tactile; close to skin, woods, incense, and musk complete the transformation.

A creative direction, not a measured consumer claim. Skin, dose, climate, and setting shape the final experience.
