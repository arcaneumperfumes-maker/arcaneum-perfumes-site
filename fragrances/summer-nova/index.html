The Story

The sun at full voltage.

Summer Nova opens with green grapefruit air—bright peel, bitter pith, and the feeling of a horizon washed clean. The light is immediate, but never weightless.

Tropical flowers rise into the heat, abstracted into radiance rather than sweetness. The center feels solar and alive, like white petals and humid leaves flashing against dark volcanic ground.

Black salt and dry timber cut through the glow. They leave a mineral, modern trail: warmth without syrup, freshness without the familiar blue.
