The Story

Softness with a current running through it.

Velvet Pulse opens with the snap of green leaves and a flash of cool air. Rose appears almost weightless—petals in motion rather than a flower arranged and still.

The brightness begins to touch skin. Suede gives the rose a fine, tactile surface while powder softens its edges without turning nostalgic. The effect is plush, but the green energy never completely disappears.

Sandalwood and musk remain as a warm blur. The fragrance becomes less an object in the air than the sensation of closeness itself.

Olfactory Journey

Spark, touch, warmth.

A bright green floral motion slows into suede, powder, and a skin-close trail.
