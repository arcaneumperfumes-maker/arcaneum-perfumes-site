The Story

Life without permission.

Wild Sovereign starts in motion. Cedrat splits open over crushed green pepper and bright stems, releasing a rush of bitter citrus, sap, and electric botanical life.

Magnolia rises through the green with a smooth, humid glow. Osmanthus brings the suggestion of apricot-toned suede—not sweet, but tactile—before incense enters like fire taking hold at the edge of the landscape.

The final warmth is human. Smoke, woods, and skin draw the wildness inward, leaving the impression of freedom worn close to the body.
