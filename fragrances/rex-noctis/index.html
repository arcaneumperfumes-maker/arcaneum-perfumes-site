The Story

Power after midnight.

Rex Noctis begins in a chamber of black stone. The air is cold, exact, almost metallic. Narcissus cuts through that stillness with a green, floral glare—beautiful, but never soft.

Then the room warms. Polished leather, shadowed oud woods, and smoke gather around the skin while musk gives the darkness a pulse. The composition moves between discipline and instinct: an immaculate silhouette with something animal breathing beneath it.

It is not a fragrance of spectacle. It is presence distilled—the feeling that the night has already chosen its sovereign.

Olfactory Journey

From frost to fire.

The structure turns a glacial opening into a dark, living trail without losing its controlled edge.

I — Opening

Mineral cold

A flash of mineral amber and green narcissus creates a severe, moonlit clarity.

II — Heart

Black polish

Leather and oud woods take form, smoothed by floral tension and a rising thread of smoke.

III — Trail

Living shadow

Musk, charred woods, and amber warmth remain close to the skin like heat held inside stone.
