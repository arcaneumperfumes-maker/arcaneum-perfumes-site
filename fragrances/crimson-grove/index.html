The Story

An orchard after frost.

Crimson Grove opens when the last light has left the forest. Fruit hangs dark and lacquered among the branches—black cherry and plum made vivid by the cold air around them.

A deep rose blooms at the center, less a bouquet than a stain of red velvet. Evergreen needles and sap push through the sweetness, keeping the orchard wild, while pale woods create the sensation of frost gathering on bark.

Amber arrives slowly, preserving the glow without turning it soft. What remains is a memory of warmth held inside a winter landscape.

Olfactory Journey

Red light, cold woods.

The fragrance holds ripe fruit and conifer air in tension, allowing neither side to become predictable.

I — Opening

Dark ripeness

Black cherry and plum appear glossy, tart-edged, and saturated rather than syrupy.

II — Heart

Crimson bloom

Dark rose meets evergreen sap, transforming fruit into a stranger, more atmospheric floral.

III — Trail

Frosted ember

Cold woods and amber preserve a restrained warmth under the green shadow of the forest.

The Impression
Opulent fruit with winter in its blood.

Crimson Grove is designed to feel lush without sweetness taking command—romantic, shadowed, and cut through with conifer cold.
