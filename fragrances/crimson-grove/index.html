The Story

An orchard after frost.

Crimson Grove opens when the last light has left the forest. Fruit hangs dark and lacquered among the branches—black cherry and plum made vivid by the cold air around them.

A deep rose blooms at the center, less a bouquet than a stain of red velvet. Evergreen needles and sap push through the sweetness, keeping the orchard wild, while pale woods create the sensation of frost gathering on bark.

Amber arrives slowly, preserving the glow without turning it soft. What remains is a memory of warmth held inside a winter landscape.
