Privacy

Your information stays part of the conversation.

Last updated August 21, 2026

ARCANEUM Perfumes is an independent fragrance house. This notice explains what information the ARCANEUM website receives when you use the private preview form and how that information is used.

Information you choose to send

When you submit the private preview form, ARCANEUM receives the name and email address you provide, your selected area of interest, your selected fragrance if any, and any optional message you write.

How ARCANEUM uses it

We use submitted information to respond to inquiries, manage private-sampling and launch-interest conversations, understand which fragrances people want to experience, and communicate about the request you made.

Infrastructure

The website uses Supabase infrastructure to receive and store private preview submissions. The public website can submit a request, but it is not given public permission to read, edit, or delete stored submissions.

Advertising and sale of information

ARCANEUM does not sell personal information submitted through this form. The current site does not use advertising trackers or third-party advertising pixels. It does load Google Fonts and uses Supabase for the private preview form.

Retention and requests

We keep inquiry information for as long as it is reasonably useful for the conversation, sampling interest, launch planning, or related business records. If you want to ask what information ARCANEUM has from your submission, correct it, or request deletion, email arcaneumperfumes@gmail.com.

Changes

If the site begins using new analytics, advertising technology, e-commerce services, or materially different data practices, this notice should be updated before those changes are relied upon.
